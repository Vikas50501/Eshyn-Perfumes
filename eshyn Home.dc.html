The story of Deghमिट्टी

The scent of first rain

Before the monsoon breaks, the air over a dry field changes. The soil gives up something it has held all summer — warm, mineral, faintly green. Degh is our attempt to keep that moment. It takes its name from the copper vessel of traditional attar making, and its character from the earth itself.
